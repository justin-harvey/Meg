Meet Meghan

Licensed Cosmetologist · Portland, Maine

I started my training on Boston's Newbury Street, and built my career over 10 years in Portland, Maine. I've been lucky to work behind the chair and as an educator in some of the top salons in major cities including Los Angeles, Las Vegas, and New York City, and have traveled all over the country for education. I did hair for Gucci! And LA Fashion Week!

All that was fun and great, but home is Maine and I'm back to create something that feels less like a salon and more like a studio — good vibes, honest conversations, and hair that actually means something to you.

Eighteen years in, I still love this work.

My specialty is vivid, dimensional color — the bold, the unexpected, the colors you've been looking at on Pinterest telling yourself "maybe someday."

If you want to express something through your hair, I'm here for it.
